UI/UX Pro Max design data

This package includes a normalized subset of design data from
nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill at Git commit
1307d97a72e6c1cda572cb65471ae5ce82995218.

The reused data is licensed under the MIT License. The full pinned license is
distributed beside this notice as LICENSE. Source-file hashes, the deterministic
repair receipt, selected columns, record counts, and canonical dataset hash are
recorded in PROVENANCE.json.

No upstream scripts, templates, CLI, installers, manifests, screenshots, previews,
documentation, premium features, host integrations, code-example columns,
documentation-URL columns, or google-fonts.csv data are included.

The craft-floor and slop-register mechanisms were independently re-expressed
after studying pbakaus/impeccable (https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), by
Paul Bakaus, Apache License 2.0, upstream commit 9d715cc. Its source files,
prose, and fixtures are not included.

The modes, review format, and routing guidance were independently re-expressed
after studying jakubkrehel/skills (https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills),
Copyright (c) 2026 Jakub Krehel, MIT License, upstream commit 267330e.
Its source and prose are not included.

The responsive and polish rules were independently re-expressed after studying
the ui-skills.com playbook from ibelick/ui-skills
(https://github.com/ibelick/ui-skills), Copyright (c) 2026 Julien Thibeaut,
MIT License. Its source and prose are not included.
