# Adversarial reasoning mode

## Grok-native contract

Select reason in the skill selector; hand approved persisted work/probes to native `start-work` and
a bounded review cycle. Conclusions remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion.
Changed evaluator or budget requires re-planning and review.

Steelman competing positions, identify shared premises, test high-leverage disagreements, and
synthesize after rebuttal. Keep evidence quality, validity, and uncertainty separate. Exhausting the
round budget is non-success, not forced consensus.
