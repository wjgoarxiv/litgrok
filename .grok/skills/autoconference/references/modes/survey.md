# Source-partitioned survey mode

## Grok-native contract

Select survey in the skill selector; hand approved retrieval/writes to the native `start-work` handoff and a subsequent approved session.
Survey artifacts remain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim family completion.
Changed evaluator or budget requires re-planning and review.

Root assigns nonoverlapping source/time/method partitions. Children return read-only packets with
queries, metadata, access evidence, claims, citation chains, uncertainty, and gaps. They never write
logs/shared files/synthesis. Root deduplicates and verifies citations; budget exhaustion is non-success.
