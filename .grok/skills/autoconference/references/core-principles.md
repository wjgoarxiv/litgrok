# Autoconference core principles

Skill selector guidance hands approved work to the native `start-work` handoff and a subsequent approved session. Family output remains
`REVIEW_REQUIRED` and cannot programmatically claim completion. Changed evaluator or budget requires
re-planning and review.

1. Root owns shared artifacts and approved changes.
2. Children are independent, read-only, packet-only, and descendant-free.
3. Claims propagate only after root evidence validation and adversarial review.
4. Knowledge transfer shares bounded claims/methods, not hidden reasoning or authority.
5. Synthesis preserves conflicts, rejected claims, and uncertainty.
6. Qualitative rubrics remain judgment, not mechanical scores.
7. Round/budget exhaustion, target evidence, and synthesis are review labels only.
