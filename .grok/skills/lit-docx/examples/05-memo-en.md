# Summary

The finance team moves from the east wing of floor 3 to the open area on floor 6 over the weekend of 14 and 15 November. Desks, monitors and locked cabinets travel with the movers; personal items go in the crates you will receive on 10 November. You start on floor 6 on Monday 16 November, with the same phone extensions and the same network drives.

# Before the move

Crates, labels and cable bags arrive at each desk on Tuesday 10 November. Pack everything you want to keep by 17:00 on Friday 13 November and label each crate with your new desk number from the floor plan sent this week. Leave your monitor, docking station and keyboard on the desk; the IT team disconnects and labels them.

Locked cabinets need one extra step. Move any cash, cheques or signed contracts into the safe in room 3.14 by Thursday evening, and hand the cabinet key to Marta Lindqvist in Workplace Services. The cabinets travel sealed, and the keys come back to you at your new desk on Monday morning.

# The weekend of the move

The movers work from 08:00 on Saturday until about 16:00 on Sunday. Floor 3 east and floor 6 are closed to staff for both days, and the goods lift is reserved for the movers. To collect something that weekend, call security on extension 2200 first.

| Day | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saturday 14 November | 08:00–13:00 | Crates and cabinets moved to floor 6 |
| Saturday 14 November | 13:00–18:00 | Desks and chairs moved and assembled |
| Sunday 15 November | 09:00–16:00 | IT connects monitors and docks, tests every desk |

# Your first week on floor 6

On Monday the IT team will be on floor 6 from 07:30 until noon to fix anything that does not work. Report problems through the usual service desk form and add the tag "floor 6 move" so they reach the right people. Reception will redirect visitors and couriers to floor 6 for the first two weeks; the payroll counter reopens there at 10:00 on Monday, in room 6.02. Quiet rooms open for booking on 9 November.

# Questions

Ask Marta Lindqvist (extension 2241) about packing and desks, and the IT service desk (extension 2000) about computers and phones. A walk-through of floor 6 starts at the lifts on Thursday 12 November at 12:30.
