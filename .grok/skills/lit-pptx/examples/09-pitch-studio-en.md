## A refill station inside a neighbourhood grocer

![Station with six products in 0.9 m of shelf width (sample) | Source: sample photo](assets/example-a.png)

- **What it is** a self-service dispenser for six cleaning products
- **How it works** customers bring any bottle, the scale reads the tare
- **Price** 25-35 % below the same product in a new bottle
- **Store fit** one shelf bay, no plumbing, restocked weekly
- **Store share** 18 % of refill revenue goes to the grocer
- Note: prices from the pilot store's shelf labels, sample figures

## Pilot results across 14 stations, March-September 2026

| Refills per station | Repeat customers | Bottles avoided | Gross margin | Store renewals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 412 / month | 58 % | 34,600 | 41 % | 13 of 14 |
| month 1: 160 | refill again within 30 days | 1-litre equivalent | after store share | at the six-month review |

- Refills per station grew every month of the pilot and had not levelled off by September
- More than half of first-time customers came back within a month
- The one store that left closed its premises for unrelated reasons
- Source: sample station telemetry and store agreements, March-September 2026 (sample)
