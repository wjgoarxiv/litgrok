## Phase 1 scope, schedule and budget

- **Summary:** phase 1 finished two weeks late and 3 % under budget; weekend use is up by a third
- **Scope:** 2.4 km of the 6.1 km trail, with 640 m of new boardwalk and 380 m of stabilised bank
- **Schedule:** 2 June to 26 September; the July high water on the south steps cost two weeks
- **Budget:** 4.62 M of 4.76 M spent: boardwalk 2.05, bank 1.34, paths 0.91, design 0.32
- **Open items:** the platform railing finish and two drainage culverts, both due in November
- Source: sample parks department project ledger and site diaries, June-September 2026 (sample)

## Bank stabilisation at the mill bend

![Stone toe and willow stakes eight weeks after planting | Source: sample photo](assets/example-b.png)

- **Problem** the bend lost about 1.2 m of bank a year to winter floods, 2019-2025
- **Works** 380 m of stone toe, 2,600 live willow stakes, coir matting on the slope
- **Early result** no measurable bank loss through the July high water (survey of 12 points)
- **Planting** 84 % of willow stakes in leaf at week eight; target 70 %
- **Next check** survey after the first winter flood, owner: river engineer

## Visitor counts and trail condition, phase 1 section

| Weekend visitors | Weekday visitors | Wheelchair passages | Path condition score | Reported hazards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,940 / day | 860 / day | 112 / week | 4.3 of 5 | 3 / month |
| 2025 same weeks: 2,210 | 2025: 790 | 2025: 18 / week | 2025: 2.6 | 2025: 14 / month |

- Weekend use rose by a third once the boardwalk reopened in late August
- Wheelchair passages grew more than sixfold after the step-free route opened
- Hazard reports fell from 14 to 3 a month, mostly loose boards and flooding
- Source: sample automatic counters at two entrances, condition survey of 40 points, Aug-Sep 2025 and 2026 (sample)

## Accessible viewing platform at the old mill

![Viewing platform over the weir, step-free from the boardwalk | Source: sample photo](assets/example-a.png)

- **Size** 6 m by 4 m, room for two wheelchairs and a bench
- **Access** level from the boardwalk, no gap over 6 mm
- **Use** about 310 visits a weekend day, sample counter
- **Open item** railing finish due 14 November
- **Cost** 0.27 M, inside the boardwalk budget
- Note: weekend visits from the sample gate counter, September and October
